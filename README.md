📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the practical implementation and understanding of Odoo ERP functional modules.

The assessment covers important business operations including:

Company configuration
Contacts and business partners
Product and product category management
CRM lead management
Opportunity management
Sales quotations
Sales orders
Inventory and delivery
Purchase RFQs
Purchase orders
Employees and departments
Customer invoicing
Customer payments
Vendor bills
Vendor credit notes
Vendor payments

The main objective is to understand how different Odoo applications work together to manage a complete business workflow from customer acquisition to sales, inventory, purchasing and accounting.
